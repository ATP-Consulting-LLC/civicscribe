To: Andover Clerk (for the Chair of the ASC AHS Future Subcommittee)
Subject: Notice of intent to record an open meeting - ASC AHS Future Subcommittee, Andover

To the Chair of the ASC AHS Future Subcommittee, care of the Andover Clerk:

This is notice under G.L. c. 30A, s. 20(c) that CivicScribe intends to make an audio recording of the open session of the ASC AHS Future Subcommittee scheduled for Thursday, September 10, 2026 at 10:00 a.m.

CivicScribe produces a searchable transcript and captions of open public meetings, so that residents who could not attend, and residents who are deaf or hard of hearing, can read what was said. Where the meeting is held in person, the recording is made by a single device placed with the clerk or at the back of the room. Nothing is attached to the body's own equipment.

The recording covers open session only. If the body votes to enter executive session, the recording stops and the assistant leaves before the executive session begins.

Section 20(c) permits recording subject to reasonable requirements as to the number, placement and operation of equipment, so as not to interfere with the conduct of the meeting. If the Chair wishes to set any such requirements, or would prefer notice for future meetings in a different form or to a different address, reply to this message and we will follow that.

A copy of this notice is retained with the recording.
